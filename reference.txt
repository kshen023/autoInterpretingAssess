Green energy is energy that has a smaller impact on the environment. Common types of green energy include solar power, wind power, and hydropower. Compared with coal and oil, these energy sources are cleaner. They can help us reduce air pollution. Today, more and more countries are starting to use green energy.

Solar power is an important type of green energy. The sun provides us with a lot of energy every day. We can use solar panels to produce electricity. Today, some families have installed solar panels on their roofs. This can not only help them save money on electricity bills, but also reduce the use of traditional energy.

In recent years, China's green energy industry has developed quickly. China produces a large number of solar panels and wind power equipment. At the same time, more and more people are buying electric cars. The government also encourages companies to use clean energy. These efforts help protect the environment and improve people's lives.

In the future, green energy will play a more and more important role. More countries will invest in solar power and wind power. Countries also need to work together and share technology and experience. Of course, developing green energy takes time and money. Everyone can also start with small things, such as saving electricity and reducing waste. Protecting the environment is our shared responsibility.
